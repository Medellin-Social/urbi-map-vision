MEDELLÍN SOCIAL – CERTIFIED REALTOR PROGRAM
Program Outline & Development Directives
Date: August 25, 2026
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
Develop the Medellín Social Certified Realtor Program as an integrated real estate marketing, technology, training, and referral ecosystem.
The program should provide participating realtors with:
* Visibility throughout Medellín Social.
* Individual realtor marketing pages.
* Property-search exposure.
* A template realtor website.
* AI-powered lead response and appointment scheduling.
* WhatsApp integration.
* Social media integrations.
* Marketing automation.
* Mobile/social Linktree-style pages.
* Training and certification.
* Access to the EASE member community.
* Participation in a Medellín referral network.
* Opportunities to generate additional referral income where legally permitted.
The development work must be separated into:
1. Medellín Social Public Website
2. EASE Membership Community & Mobile App
3. Realtor Technology Package & Workflow
4. Training and Certification
5. Referral Network
6. Pricing, Offer, Testing, and Launch
________________


PART I — MEDELLÍN SOCIAL PUBLIC WEBSITE
2. INDIVIDUAL REALTOR LISTING/PAGE
Directive
Complete the mapping and development framework for a standardized individual realtor page.
Every participating/certified realtor should have an individual featured page within Medellín Social.
The page should be designed as a reusable template so new certified realtors can be added efficiently.
Page should accommodate:
* Realtor name.
* Professional photograph.
* Brokerage/company.
* Contact information.
* Biography.
* Areas served.
* Specialties.
* Languages.
* Social media profiles.
* WhatsApp contact.
* Call/contact CTA.
* Appointment scheduling.
* Realtor's property listings.
* Relevant certifications.
* Medellín Social certification/status.
* Links to the realtor's individual website.
* Mobile/social profile page.
* Lead capture.
Referral Opportunity Feature
Investigate and design an optional section allowing the realtor to indicate that they accept referrals for:
* Buyers.
* Sellers.
* Investors.
* Renters or other applicable prospects.
The system should potentially allow the realtor to advertise a referral opportunity or referral compensation.
IMPORTANT: This functionality should not be activated publicly until the legality and regulatory requirements surrounding referral compensation in Colombia have been researched and confirmed.

Directive

2. A property-search landing page.

PART II — REALTOR TECHNOLOGY PACKAGE
7. REALTOR TEMPLATE WEBSITE

Develop one standardized realtor website template that can be duplicated and provisioned for each qualifying realtor.
Access to this website will be included as part of the realtor's certification/program package.
The objective is NOT to custom-build an entirely different website for every realtor.
Create a scalable template that can be quickly personalized using each realtor's:
* Name.
* Branding.
* Photograph.
* Contact information.
* Biography.
* Social profiles.
* WhatsApp.
* Listings.
* Service areas.
* Lead forms.
* Appointment calendar.
Determine what information can be automatically populated during onboarding.

8. AI REALTOR AGENT
Directive
Each participating realtor should receive access to an AI agent capable of assisting with incoming opportunities.
The system should be configured to support:
* Answering incoming calls.
* Basic prospect qualification.
* Answering common questions.
* Capturing prospect information.
* Scheduling appointments.
* Routing appropriate conversations to the realtor.
* Triggering appropriate follow-up.
Develop a standardized realtor AI-agent configuration that can be duplicated and customized during onboarding.

________________


15. PROFESSIONAL-SERVICE REFERRAL RESEARCH
Before activating referral compensation involving regulated or professional services, conduct a separate legal/compliance review for Colombia.
Research should specifically address referral compensation involving:
* Attorneys/legal services.
* Mortgage/lending.
* Banking/financial services.
* Insurance.
* Real estate transactions.
* Other regulated professions.
Determine:
* Who may legally receive referral compensation.
* Who may legally pay it.
* Whether disclosure is required.
* Whether compensation can be advertised publicly.
* Whether licensing or registration is required.
* Whether different rules apply to consumers versus licensed professionals.
Do not assume U.S. referral rules apply in Colombia.

22. CORE PROGRAM CONCEPT
The Medellín Social Certified Realtor Program should ultimately operate as an ecosystem rather than simply a realtor directory.
A participating realtor should receive:
Visibility + Website + AI + WhatsApp + Social Media + Automation + Training + Certification + Community + Referral Network + Business Development Opportunities
Every development decision should support making this package scalable so that a new realtor can be enrolled, provisioned, trained, certified, published, and activated without rebuilding the system from scratch.
